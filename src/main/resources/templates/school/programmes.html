======= Secondary School Programmes - Christian International School (CIS)
Academic Pathways (S1 – S6)

CIS Secondary School Programmes

CIS offers a structured six-year secondary curriculum (S1–S6) with two internationally recognized graduation pathways tailored to diverse university aspirations.

Two-Stage Secondary Progression

Stage 1: S1 – S3

Junior Secondary Foundation

Builds core competencies across English, Chinese, Mathematics, Integrated Sciences, Humanities, and Coding, preparing students for smooth transition into either DSE or IB streams.

Stage 2: S4 – S6

Senior Secondary Specialization

Students specialize in either the HKDSE Programme or the IB Programme, supported by dedicated university guidance counselors for Hong Kong, UK, US, and global admissions.

Local & International Recognition S1 – S6 Track

HKDSE Programme

Hong Kong Diploma of Secondary Education

Designed for students seeking strong bilingual proficiency and direct JUPAS admission to Hong Kong's UGC-funded universities as well as overseas institutions.

  • ✓ Core Subjects: English Language, Chinese Language, Mathematics, Citizenship and Social Development.
  • ✓ Elective Flexibility: Select 2 to 3 electives across STEM, Business, and Humanities.
  • ✓ Medium of Instruction: English (except Chinese Language & Chinese History).

Global University Pathway S1 – S6 Track

IB Programme

International Baccalaureate Middle Years & Diploma Pathway

An inquiry-driven international framework emphasizing critical thinking, interdisciplinary research, and global citizenship for leading universities worldwide.

  • ✓ Six Subject Groups: Studies in Language & Literature, Language Acquisition, Individuals & Societies, Sciences, Mathematics, and Arts.
  • ✓ DP Core: Theory of Knowledge (TOK), Extended Essay (EE), and Creativity, Activity, Service (CAS).
  • ✓ Assessment: Balanced internal coursework assessments and external examinations.
