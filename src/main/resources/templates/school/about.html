01 / School Background

Our Heritage & Educational Vision

Christian International School (CIS) is a newly established co-educational secondary institution in Hong Kong, dedicated to providing world-class education from Secondary 1 (S1) to Secondary 6 (S6) with a full capacity of approximately 1,000 students.

Grounded in Christian principles of integrity, compassion, and service, CIS bridges Eastern cultural heritage with international academic rigor. Our dual-pathway curriculum prepares students for both local and global top-tier universities.

Levels Offered S1 – S6
