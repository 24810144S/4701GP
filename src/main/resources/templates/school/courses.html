======= Course Descriptions - Christian International School (CIS)
Curriculum & Syllabi

Course Descriptions by Programme

Detailed subject outlines and academic modules offered under the HKDSE and IB programmes across junior (S1–S3) and senior (S4–S6) secondary levels.

1. HKDSE Programme — Course Outlines

Core & Elective Subject Descriptions (S1–S6)

Apply for HKDSE
Core

English Language & Literature (DSE-ENG)

Develops advanced academic reading, argumentative writing, listening, and public speaking skills. Includes School-Based Assessment (SBA) modules on literary texts and contemporary media.

Core

Chinese Language & Cultural Studies (DSE-CHI)

Covers classical Chinese prose, modern literature analysis, practical writing, and oral communication, strengthening bilingual fluency and cultural literacy.

Core + Extended

Mathematics & Calculus / Statistics M1 & M2 (DSE-MAT)

Compulsory part spans algebra, geometry, trigonometry, and probability. Senior students may take Extended Part Module 1 (Calculus & Statistics) or Module 2 (Algebra & Calculus).

Electives

STEM Sciences, ICT & Humanities Electives

Students select 2–3 electives from Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Information & Communication Technology (ICT), Economics, Business Accounting & Financial Studies (BAFS), and World History.

2. IB Programme — Course Outlines

IB Subject Groups & Diploma Core (S1–S6)

Apply for IB
Group 1 & 2

Studies in Language & Literature / Language Acquisition

Offered at Higher Level (HL) and Standard Level (SL) in English A: Literature, Chinese A: Language & Literature, and French/Spanish B, fostering critical literary appreciation.

Group 3 & 4

Individuals & Societies / Experimental Sciences

Includes Economics, Global Politics, Psychology, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Computer Science. Incorporates laboratory Internal Assessments (IA) and collaborative science projects.

Group 5 & 6

Mathematics (AA / AI) & Visual Arts / Music

Offers Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches (AA) for theoretical/STEM tracks and Applications and Interpretation (AI) for data-driven modeling, alongside Visual Arts and Music.

IB Core

Theory of Knowledge (TOK), Extended Essay (EE) & CAS

Mandatory core components cultivating epistemology inquiry (TOK), a 4,000-word independent academic research paper (EE), and experiential community service (CAS).
