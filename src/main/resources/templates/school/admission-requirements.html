Entry Criteria

HKDSE Programme Requirements

  • • Academic Standing: Satisfactory academic performance in the previous two school years, with at least Grade B / Level 4 equivalent in English, Chinese, and Mathematics.
  • • Entrance Assessment: Written placement tests in English Language and Mathematics (applicable to S1–S5 transfers).
  • • Admission Interview: Individual bilingual interview assessing communication skills, conduct, and alignment with CIS school values.
Entry Criteria

IB Programme Requirements

  • • Academic Standing: Strong academic transcripts demonstrating analytical and inquiry skills across languages, sciences, and mathematics.
  • • English Proficiency: High proficiency in academic English writing and comprehension (CEFR B2 / IELTS 6.0 equivalent for senior entry).
  • • Admission Interview: English panel interview and critical-thinking portfolio review for S4–S6 Diploma applicants.
